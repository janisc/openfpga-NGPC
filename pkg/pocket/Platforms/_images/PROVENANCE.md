# Platform image provenance

ngpc.bin is derived from "Neo-Geo-Pocket-Color-Blue-Left.jpg" by Evan-Amos
(Vanamo Media), released into the public domain worldwide:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Neo-Geo-Pocket-Color-Blue-Left.jpg

Processing: background cut to alpha, grayscale, wide crop through the
device face, 521x165, encoded as the Pocket platform-image format
(headerless 16-bit gray+alpha, stored rotated -90 degrees -- format
reverse-engineered from existing platform images and cross-checked against
community tooling). Source photo and intermediates in assets/ (the 6 MB
original is not committed; the URL above is canonical).
