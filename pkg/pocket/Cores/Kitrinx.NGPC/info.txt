Neo Geo Pocket / Neo Geo Pocket Color

Port of the MiSTer core by Kitrinx (Jamie Blanks) to openFPGA.

Place the BIOS images in Assets/ngpc/Kitrinx.NGPC/:
  boot0.rom   Color BIOS (64 KiB)
  boot1.rom   Mono BIOS (64 KiB)

Work in progress: cartridge loading, saves and sleep are not implemented yet.
